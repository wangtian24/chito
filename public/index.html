citoVersion 0.1.0

A simple web UI for Claude Code. It lists the sessions on this machine, shows each one as a conversation, gives every session and message a permalink, and reopens a session in your terminal.

It runs locally and reads the transcripts in ~/.claude/projects. Nothing is sent anywhere.

cito is open source and public under the MIT license. Code, issues and changes live at github.com/wangtian24/cito.
